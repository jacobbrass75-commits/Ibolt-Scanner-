iBOLT Scan

Warehouse inventory, ready to count.

iBOLT Scan helps the warehouse team look up parts, scan barcodes, record bin weights, and save physical inventory counts.

What you can do

  • Find products in the shared inventory catalog.
  • Use a compatible barcode scanner to look up a part or bin.
  • Use measured item and empty-bin weights to calculate quantities.
  • Save counts with an operator name and review count history.

Sign in to use the shared inventory

Registration is open. New accounts can work with the shared warehouse inventory, so changes and saved counts should reflect real inventory work.

If you choose Google sign-in, iBOLT Scan uses basic account information through Clerk to identify you. It does not request access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, or contacts.
